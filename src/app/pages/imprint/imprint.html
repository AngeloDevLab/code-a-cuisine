AI generated recipes

All recipes are generated automatically by an AI (Google Gemini) and are not checked by a human. They can contain mistakes, for example in quantities, cooking times, temperatures or nutrition values. Please check every recipe before cooking, cook meat, fish and eggs thoroughly, and pay attention to allergies and intolerances yourself.
