2. Overview

Code à Cuisine is a learning project that generates recipes from your ingredients with an AI. There are no accounts, no cookies and no tracking. Personal data is only processed to deliver the website, to limit the number of recipe requests per user and day, and to remember your likes.

3. Hosting of the website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open a page, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, the time of access and the requested page, to deliver the website and protect it against attacks.

Provider: GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a secure, working website).

4. Recipe generation and daily limit

When you generate recipes, your browser sends your ingredients, your preferences (portions, cooking time, cuisine, diet, number of cooks) and a random browser id to our automation server (n8n). The server reads your IP address from the request.

Each request uses a paid AI service, so every user may send 3 requests per day. To count them, your IP address and your browser id are stored together with the time of the request. Both are used for nothing else and are deleted automatically after 2 days.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in limiting costs and preventing abuse).

5. AI service (Google Gemini)

To generate the recipes, the server sends your ingredients and preferences to the Google Gemini API. Your IP address and your browser id are never sent to Google.

Provider: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. We use the paid tier: under Google's terms, submitted content is not used to improve Google's products and is only stored for a limited period to detect abuse. Please do not enter personal information in the ingredient fields.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (generating the recipes you asked for).

6. Public cookbook

Every generated recipe is saved and shown to all visitors in the public cookbook. A recipe contains your ingredients and preferences, but no IP address, no browser id and no other reference to you. Likes are counted as a plain number per recipe.

7. Database (Supabase)

Recipes, likes and the request log of the daily limit are stored in a Supabase database in Frankfurt, Germany (region eu-central-1). When you open the cookbook or a recipe, or like a recipe, your browser loads the data directly from Supabase, which processes your IP address to deliver it.

Provider: Supabase Pte. Ltd., 65 Chulia Street #38-02/03, OCBC Centre, Singapore 049513. Supabase uses EU Standard Contractual Clauses as a safeguard for transfers outside the EU. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

8. Automation server (Hetzner)

The automation software n8n runs on a server of Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany, located in Germany. For troubleshooting, n8n keeps the data of each request, including your IP address and browser id, for at most 14 days and then deletes it automatically.

9. Local storage in your browser

The app stores two entries in the local storage of your browser:

  • client-id: the random browser id for the daily limit
  • liked-recipes: the recipes you liked, so you can only like each recipe once

These entries are not cookies and are never read by third parties. They stay until you clear the site data of your browser. Legal basis: § 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG, as they are necessary for the functions you use.

10. No cookies, no tracking

This app uses no cookies, no analytics tools and no tracking. Fonts and icons are hosted with the website, there is no connection to Google Fonts or similar services.

11. Storage duration

  • Request log (IP address, browser id): 2 days
  • Data of each request on the automation server: at most 14 days
  • Recipes and likes: without time limit, as they contain no personal data
  • Local storage: until you clear it
